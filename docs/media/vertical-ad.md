# Vertical video ad

[`vertical-ad.mp4`](vertical-ad.mp4) is a 24-second English ad for TikTok and
YouTube Shorts: 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC stereo, approximately 5.1 MiB.

The first 20 seconds show actual gameplay recorded from main at `1595a230`
with seed 7: telescope launches, three-star tap links, payouts, and Sun
rekindling. No gameplay rules, rewards, balance, or progress were modified for
the recording. The last four seconds show the Scorpio painting, game title,
and **DOWNLOAD NOW / GOOGLE PLAY / APP STORE**, as requested by the owner.
The store names are plain typeset text, not official store badges. Store links
were not supplied; add them to the social post or paid-ad destination settings.
This task does not upload or publish the ad.

Captions use the existing 5x7 bitmap glyphs and Stellar Sun palette. Composition
is drawn at 216 x 384 and enlarged exactly 5x with nearest-neighbor filtering;
the entire 180 x 320 game view remains visible. Encoded video naturally has
compression-related color variation. Audio combines the actual game capture
with a quiet original synthesized pentatonic cue, generated by the build script.
There is no voiceover or third-party music.

## Rebuild

Requires Godot 4.7, Python with Pillow, and FFmpeg on PATH. From the repo root:

```powershell
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force output/media | Out-Null
godot --headless --editor --import --quit --log-file output/media/import.log
godot --path . --log-file output/media/capture.log --windowed --resolution 540x960 --fixed-fps 30 --write-movie output/media/gameplay.avi --script tools/media/capture_ad.gd
python tools/media/build_ad.py
```

The builder writes the MP4, poster, review sheet, original musical cue and
intermediate frames under `output/media/ad/`. `--out PATH` changes the output
directory; `--cta "COMING SOON"` produces a pre-release variant. The capture
bot uses supported input handlers and valid links; it never injects stars or
forces a Big Bang. Aim coordinates and timings are capture choreography,
not game tuning.

## Validation

- Godot rendered 960 gameplay frames at 30 fps.
- Reviewed chronological stills covering launch, linking, reward particles,
  Sun ignition, and the store card.
- FFprobe confirmed 24.000 seconds, 1080 x 1920, H.264, 30 fps, AAC stereo at 48 kHz.
- FFmpeg decoded the entire final MP4 without errors.
- No gameplay or balance files changed; gameplay unit tests and the balance
  simulator are not applicable to this media-only change.

The recording environment logged an unavailable system certificate store and
shutdown resource warnings after successfully writing the movie. Neither
prevented capture or affected final-file decoding.
